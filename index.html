Pricing Guide & 20% Surge Profit Calculator

Understand how Dynamic & Fixed pricing works for your farm

How Dynamic Daily Market Peg Works

TAZA pegs your crop price to the daily Mandi (wholesale market) benchmark. Each day, a market swing factor (±5.5%) is computed from today's date and your crop name. Your final listing price moves with the market — protecting you from stale pricing while keeping buyers confident in fair rates.

Advantages for Farmers
  • 20% Profit Share Bonus — when wholesale Mandi prices spike, you automatically earn 20% of that surge directly on top of your base price.
  • MSP Floor Protection — set a minimum price floor so you never sell below your breakeven cost, even when market dips.
  • Price reflects real supply & demand, building trust with urban consumers who compare Mandi rates.
Considerations
  • Price is variable day-to-day — consumer sees a slightly different rate each morning.
  • Requires trust that TAZA's benchmark data is accurate (verified against public Mandi records).

How Fixed Farm Gate Price Works

You set a single constant price per kg that remains unchanged regardless of daily Mandi movements. Consumers see the same price every day. Simple, transparent, and highly predictable — great for long-term buyer relationships.

Advantages
  • Full predictability — you know exactly what you'll earn before harvest.
  • Easier for consumers — they can plan grocery budgets without price surprises.
  • Ideal for subscription orders & bulk B2B buyers who need cost certainty.
What You Miss
  • No 20% profit-share bonus during Mandi price spikes — you earn a flat rate even when market surges.
  • Price may feel undervalued to consumers during low-market days (they see Mandi is higher).
